## ✨ Features

### Admin
- **Live Dashboard** – users, signups, revenue, subscribers, pending earnings, payouts, releases
- **Release Management** – approve/reject, filter by status, track copyright
- **Metadata Editor & Exporter** – fix ISRC/UPC/credits; one-click ZIP (JSON + CSV + PDF) + audio/cover downloads
- **Royalties & CSV Import** – Soundrop + auto-detect parsers with duplicate protection
- **Earnings Approval** – approve/reject/mass-approve earnings and credit artist wallets
- **Withdrawals** – approve/reject payout requests with history
- **Subscription & Pricing** – artist/label plans with per-plan pricing
- **Homepage Content Manager** – edit the entire landing page without touching code
- **Six Payment Gateways** – PayPal, Paystack, CoinPayments, NowPayments, MoneyUnify, Manual
- **Currency Management** – admin-configurable conversion rates

### Artist & Label
- **Release Upload Wizard** – Singles / EPs / Albums with 3000×3000px cover validation
- **Chunked Audio Uploads** – large WAV/MP3 masters on shared hosting
- **Multi-Track Management** – titles, featured artists, producers, credits, ISRC
- **Automatic Copyright Scan** – ACRCloud fingerprinting with free tier
- **Streaming Analytics** – plays, earnings, trends per release/track
- **Royalties & Wallet** – approved earnings + live wallet balance
- **Label Roster** – manage multiple artists, upload on their behalf, centralized royalties

### Payments & Security
- **Signature-verified webhooks** – idempotent callbacks; one payment = one activation
- **Private master storage** – audio stored outside the public web root, served only via authorized endpoints
- **4-step web installer** – no Composer/SSH required (vendor included)
